## What competitors and the category run (Miami and US home services)

- A price plus a season plus "Book now" is the most common HVAC format (for example, a $59 to $149 tune-up).
- Cross-trade bundles: an AC tune-up with a free plumbing inspection, which has run for months (a signal of performance).
- A real Google review screenshot over a photo of the branded van performs better than a designed graphic.
- Owner-on-camera and phone-shot vertical video. Maintenance-plan videos report $30 to $60 CPL.
- Local Miami offers: $99 to $149 tune-ups (31-point check, drain cleaning), and a free pound of refrigerant.
- The public Ad Library no longer shows engagement, so the run time of an ad is the only public signal.

## 10 concepts

### Awareness (campaign 1, $12/day)

**A1 · Neighbors** (based on the Spanish "Neighbors" ad, $51 CPL)
Map of the 21 ZIP codes with pins on real jobs. EN: "Your neighbors in Coral Gables, Pinecrest and Kendall already trust us." ES: "Tus vecinos en Coral Gables, Pinecrest y Kendall ya confían en nosotros." Format: 9:16 video, 10 seconds.

**A2 · Real review** (category pattern)
Three Google reviews as screenshots over a photo of the Sunny Bliss van. EN: "South Miami's top-rated plumbing and AC team. 4.9 stars, real homeowners." Format: carousel, three cards.

**A3 · Owner** (owner-on-camera pattern)
Mehdi speaks to camera for 15 seconds: "When you call Sunny Bliss, a licensed tech calls you back in five minutes. Bilingual, licensed and local." Format: Reel, recorded on a phone.

### Leads, prospecting ($40/day)

**L1 · Tank Flush $149** (best plumbing ad, 17 leads, $48 CPL)
EN: "Water heater flush, $149. Hot water lasts longer and the tank lives years more." ES version included. Winter angle: hot water use is higher. Reuse the Sep 24 creative and add the Noel tankless video ($289) as a second asset.

**L2 · 0% for 60 months** (best ad in the account, $27.72 CPL in Spanish)
Reuse the main video. ES first: "Aire nuevo desde $0 de entrada y 0% de interés por 60 meses." EN version included. Form question: "How old is your AC?"

**L3 · 15-year-old AC** (25-year-old AC video, $55.60 CPL)
EN: "Is your AC 15 years or older? Get a free replacement estimate before it fails." Hook: the real age of the unit. Subtitle with the price from $5,800.

**L4 · Tune-up plus plumbing check** (competitor bundle pattern)
EN: "Winter AC tune-up plus a free plumbing inspection. One visit, two systems checked." ES: "Mantenimiento del aire más revisión de plomería gratis." Price [$__] approved by Mehdi. Bliss Care Plan on the confirmation screen.

**L5 · Installed this week** (hyperlocal Spanish ad, $33.96 CPL)
Photo of a real installation with the neighborhood name. EN: "New AC installed this week in Pinecrest. From $5,800 with 0% financing." One version per area: Coral Gables, Kendall and Miami Beach.

**L6 · Rainy winter** (El Niño, wet winter expected)
EN: "A wet winter is coming. Drain and leak inspection before the rain, [$__]." Photo of a technician with a drain camera. It opens the plumbing service in a season with low AC demand.

### Leads, retargeting ($8/day)

**R1 · Bliss Care Plan** (maintenance-plan video pattern, $30 to $60 CPL)
EN: "One plan, two visits a year: AC and plumbing. Priority service and no dispatch fee." It reaches people who visited the website or watched the awareness videos.

**R2 · Before you sign** (second-opinion angle)
EN: "Got a quote for a new AC? Get a second one free before you sign." It reaches visitors to the financing and replacement pages.
