Frequently asked questions

What does the Black Friday offer include?

$0 upfront (no setup or placement fee) and 50% off Orchid's first monthly service fee, for calls booked by December 1st at 11:59 pm ET, while spots last.

Are they HIPAA-trained?

Yes. Every professional placed with a healthcare practice completes HIPAA training and certification before starting, and Orchid renews it every year.

What support do you give after they start?

Ongoing HR support from Orchid: regular check-ins with your assistant, coaching, yearly HIPAA renewal and a $0 replacement if it's not the right fit. You manage the work; we take care of the people side.

How do you select them?

Our HR team interviews every candidate personally, runs a behavioral assessment and a live English assessment, and checks references one by one. No AI filters.

How much does it cost after the first month?

It depends on the role and schedule. Most full-time roles start around $1,700 to $2,200 per month, all-in. You get an exact number on the call.
