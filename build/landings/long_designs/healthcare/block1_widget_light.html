Frequently asked questions

How do you make sure my practice stays HIPAA compliant?

Every professional we place in a healthcare role completes HIPAA training before they start, signs confidentiality and PHI-handling agreements, and works only inside your systems with the minimum access you grant. We're ready to sign a BAA, and our HR team supervises every placement. Your practice keeps full control over access and can revoke it anytime.

Will patient data ever leave my systems?

No patient data should live outside your systems. Your professional works inside your EHR, phone and scheduling tools, never stores PHI on personal devices, and follows your security policies.

Why does Spanish-speaking support matter for my practice?

Spanish is the most spoken language in the U.S. after English, and millions of patients have limited English proficiency. When they can communicate in their own language, they book, show up, follow their treatment and refer their families. When they can't, they go elsewhere or skip care altogether.
